🦴 The skeleton's screen kit

Every ghost-run app wears the same screens.

Worlds bring the data. These parts draw it, the same way in every app. They never decide anything: the engine judges, the kit only shows what it was given.

01

Mirror world

Reality on the left, the ghost's copy on the right, one row per change.

  • Deleted files, overwritten files and lost rows are marked in red.
  • Fields that look like secrets are hidden as [hidden: secret-like field].
  • Unrecognized changes are shown, never dropped.
02

Candle

The verdict at a glance. The claims under it say what this run proved.

  • Pass: lit, every ward cleared.
  • Warn: flickering, a human must approve.
  • Block: out, with the ward and the numbers.
03

Blacklight

Turn it on and the hidden letters glow.

  • Finds zero-width and direction-control characters, and HTML comments.
  • Shows where they sit, line and column.
  • Says plainly what text analysis cannot catch.
04

Red thread

From the blocked step to the line that put the idea in the agent's head.

  • Links only literal matches, never guesses.
  • "Not found" is said out loud, and never means safe.
05

Graveyard

Every plan the wards stopped rests here.

  • One tombstone per blocked command, with its ward and time.
  • Open a tombstone to read the seance: what was tried and why it stopped.
06

Lights on

The same screens for bright rooms and printouts.

  • Every color keeps its meaning: red still stops, green still goes.
  • Contrast rules are checked in both themes.

Base controls

Buttons and inputs that match the kit, so an app's own form fits in.
